## Audio Content Overview

### Section 1: General Intro (10s)
Introduction to ProcureX as an AI-powered procurement platform with smart automation and real-time insights.

### Section 2: Dashboard (5s)
Comprehensive overview dashboard with real-time metrics and quick module access.

### Section 3: Organisation Registration (5s)
Seamless setup and configuration of procurement entities.

### Section 4: User Registration (5s)
Role-based user access management with secure authentication.

### Section 5: Farmer Registration (10s)
Streamlined farmer onboarding with profile management, land registration, scheme allocation, and analytics.

### Section 6: Lot Entry (10s)
Procurement transaction management with quantity tracking, quality assessment, and payment processing.

### Section 7: Center Dispatch (10s)
Warehouse operations with shipment tracking, transportation management, and dispatch reporting.

### Section 8: WHR Creation (10s)
Digital Warehouse Receipt creation with inventory tracking and quality verification.

### Section 9: Inventory Management (10s)
Real-time stock visibility, warehouse operations, and comprehensive inventory analytics.

### Section 10: AI Reporting Dashboard (20s)
Natural language query interface with predictive analytics, anomaly detection, and machine learning insights.

### Section 11: Unified Reporting Dashboard (30s)
Consolidated reporting interface combining 30+ traditional reports into four main categories: Daily Operations, Farmer Analytics, Procurement Analytics, and Payment Analytics.

### Section 12: Setting Master Screen (30s)
Comprehensive system configuration including centers, warehouses, commodities, limits, schemes, and procurement rules.
